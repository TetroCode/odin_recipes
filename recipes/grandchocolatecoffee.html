Grand Chocolate Coffee

Odin Recipes

Description

An elegant harmonizing of the flavors of coffee, citrus, and chocolate into a sumptuous flavor experience for the palate.

Ingredients

  • Two shots of freshly brewed espresso
  • Grand Marnier
  • Bourbon
  • Chocolate sauce
  • Ice cubes
  • One orange twist

Steps

  1. Brew two shots of espresso (1.5 fl. oz. each) into a small pitcher
  2. Stir one teaspoon of chocolate sauce into the pitcher and stir until it melts
  3. Place 4-5 ice cubes in the glass
  4. Pour chocolate-espresso mix over the ice cubes. Stir until the espresso cools
  5. Add one tablespoon of Grand Marnier and 1.5 fl. oz. of Bourbon
  6. Garnish with one orange twist and serve
