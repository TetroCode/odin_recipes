Grilled Zander with Spring Vegetables á la grecque

Odin Recipes

Description

Freshly caught, from cool Norwegian waters, a Zander fish fillet accompanied by Norwegian spring vegetables, in the style of the Greeks.

Ingredients

  • 1 aubergine
  • 1 red pepper
  • 8 mushrooms
  • 2 stalks celery
  • 1/2 red chili
  • 1 red onion
  • 4 cloves of garlic
  • 100 ml of olive oil
  • 200 ml of white wine
  • 8 small tomatoes
  • 600-800 g of zander fillets, scaled but with skin
  • Salt
  • Black Pepper
  • Dill
  • Fresh coriander leaves
  • A few chives

Steps

  1. Start by chopping the aubergines, pepper, mushrooms, and celery into 2 cm pieces.
  2. Halve the chili lengthwise and slice.
  3. Peel and slice the onions and garlic.
  4. Fry all the vegetables in olive oil, then add the white wine, and simmer until al dente.
  5. Remove from heat, halve the tomatoes, add to the pan, and let sit.
  6. Portion the zander into large-ish pieces, brush with a little olive oil, and season with salt and pepper.
  7. Sear in hot frying pan, skin-side down, for about two minutes on each side.
  8. To serve: stir the dill, coriander, and chives into the vegetables. Arrange on a deep platter and place the fish on top.
