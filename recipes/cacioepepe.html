Cacio e Pepe

Odin Recipes

Description

Fresh pasta supports and melds the rich, creamy flavors of the classic Roman dish Cacio e Pepe

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti - high quality pasta
  • Black Pepper - freshly ground
  • Pecorino Romano Cheese - good quality, freshly grated
  • Parmesan Cheese - good quality, freshly grated

Steps

  1. Boil the water in a large pot, add the salt and the spaghetti.
  2. While the pasta is cooking, ground the pepper corns until you have the desired amount of pepper.
  3. About 3 minutes before the time is up (2 minutes less then directed) in a large skillet add the freshly ground pepper and heat on high for about 30 seconds or until you can smell the pepper, add about half a cup of hot pasta water (you need the starchy pasta water which helps to prevent clump).
  4. Cook on high, stirring constantly for about 30-45 seconds, it will be almost melted but some water will remain. Remove from the heat.
  5. In a small bowl add the pecorino and a little parmesan, combine, add half a cup (or a little more if needed) of hot pasta water and stir to make a creamy sauce.
  6. By this time your pasta is ready (a little less than al dente). Place the skillet with the pepper sauce back on to medium-high heat, add the spaghetti and the pecorino cream sauce, quickly toss and stir gently to combine. Add a little pasta water if needed and continue to combine.
  7. When the spaghetti is cooked (about a minute) and the creamy sauce has thickened slightly, the Cacio e Pepe is ready. Divide into 2-3 plates, top with a sprinkle of black pepper and extra pecorino cheese.
